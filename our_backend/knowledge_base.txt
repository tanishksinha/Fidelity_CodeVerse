## About Synaptic Wealth
Synaptic Wealth is a next-generation AI-powered financial advisory platform built for modern Indian investors. We combine behavioral analytics, machine-learning portfolio optimization, and real-time market intelligence to help you invest smarter. Our platform is fully SEBI-registered and regulated.

## Account Types & Minimums
- **Starter Plan:** Minimum investment ₹1,000. No lock-in. Access to curated mutual funds and index ETFs.
- **Growth Plan:** Minimum investment ₹25,000. Includes SIP automation, tax-loss harvesting, and quarterly advisor calls.
- **Wealth Plan:** Minimum investment ₹5,00,000. Dedicated human advisor, bespoke portfolio construction, priority support, and estate planning guidance.
- All plans are interchangeable — you can upgrade or downgrade at any time without penalties.

## Fees & Charges
- **Advisory Fee:** 0.5% per annum on Assets Under Management (AUM) for Starter; 0.35% for Growth; 0.20% for Wealth.
- **Transaction Fee:** Zero. We do not charge per trade or per SIP installment.
- **Withdrawal Fee:** Zero. Withdrawals process within 1-3 business days with no exit load from Synaptic's side (fund-level exit loads may apply per SEBI regulations for the first year on certain ELSS funds).
- **GST:** 18% GST is applicable on the advisory fee as per Government of India regulations.
- There are NO hidden fees, no account maintenance charges, and no minimum balance penalties.

## KYC (Know Your Customer) Process
- KYC is mandatory under SEBI and PMLA regulations for all investment accounts.
- **Documents Required:**
  1. Government-issued Photo ID: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License.
  2. PAN Card (mandatory for investments above ₹50,000 per year).
  3. A selfie or live photo for liveness verification.
  4. Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement) for fund settlement.
- **How to upload:** Go to the KYC section, tap "Upload Document," select the document type, and either take a live photo or upload from gallery. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, PDF. Maximum file size: 5 MB per document.
- **Common Upload Issues:**
  - If the "Submit ID" button is unresponsive, ensure your file is under 5 MB and in a supported format.
  - If the button shows a spinner indefinitely, try refreshing the page and re-uploading. Your previously uploaded documents are saved.
  - iOS Safari users: enable camera permissions in Settings → Safari → Camera.
  - If errors persist, use our live chat or email support@synaptic.ai.
- **KYC Verification Time:** Typically 1-2 business days via CKYC registry. Express verification (same-day) is available for Wealth Plan members.
- E-KYC via Aadhaar OTP is available for instant verification without document upload — look for the "Verify with Aadhaar" option.

## Common Technical Issues & Resolutions
- **App not loading:** Clear browser cache and cookies, or try incognito mode.
- **Login issues:** Use "Forgot Password" to reset via email OTP. Accounts are locked after 5 failed attempts for 30 minutes.
- **OTP not received:** Check spam folder; wait 60 seconds and request a new OTP; ensure your registered mobile number is active.
- **Portfolio not updating:** Portfolio NAVs update once daily after market close (around 10:00 PM IST). Intraday prices are indicative only.
- **SIP failure:** If a SIP installment fails due to insufficient balance, it is retried once. After two failures, the SIP is paused — you can resume it from the SIP section.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my money safe with Synaptic?**
A: Yes. Your investments are held directly in your name in your demat/folio account — Synaptic never holds your funds. We act as your advisor, not a custodian. Even if Synaptic ceased operations, your investments would remain intact and accessible.

**Q: Can I invest in US stocks?**
A: Currently, we offer US-market exposure through International Fund of Funds (FoF) listed in India. Direct US stock trading is on our roadmap for Q3 2025.

**Q: How does the AI advisor work?**
A: Our AI analyzes your risk tolerance, investment horizon, tax bracket, and existing portfolio to recommend optimal fund allocations. Recommendations are reviewed by certified advisors before being surfaced to you.

**Q: What is the lock-in period?**
A: Only ELSS (tax-saving) funds have a mandatory 3-year lock-in under Section 80C. All other fund categories have no lock-in on our platform.

**Q: Can I have joint accounts?**
A: Joint accounts are currently not supported. This feature is planned for Q4 2025.

**Q: How do I close my account?**
A: Redeem all your investments first, then go to Settings → Account → Close Account. Closure is processed within 5 business days.
